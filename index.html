A Georgian Manor in the English Countryside

A wedding day entirely your own.

A beautiful old manor, a table full of your favourite people, and the freedom to make the day feel unmistakably yours. Just one wedding, and the whole place to yourselves.

Picture Your Day Explore the Manor

Exclusive Use

One address.
All yours.

When you choose Ashden Manor, you are not booking a function room in a hotel. You are taking the whole place — the manor, the gardens and the Georgian dining room — for your wedding day.

There is one wedding at a time here. No unrelated hotel guests wandering through the hall, no sharing the gardens with another celebration, no feeling that your day is one of several happening at once. The house is yours from the moment you arrive.

That privacy changes the atmosphere. Speeches can run long. Children can play on the lawn. Your guests can move between the ceremony room, the dining room and the gardens without ever leaving your bubble.

One Wedding at a Time

One hundred day guests is not a restriction — it is an invitation to spend real time with the people you love most. With the whole manor to yourselves, the day feels full without feeling crowded, and the evening celebration can welcome up to 150 guests in total.

The Spaces

Different spaces. One lovely day.

Each room has its own character, but they belong to the same story. You move through them as the day unfolds — from vows, to dinner, to dancing and quiet moments in the gardens.

The Ceremony Room

An intimate indoor setting where the atmosphere does the work. Close enough for every guest to feel part of the moment, and quiet enough to hear every word.

The Georgian Dining Room

A beautiful room for shared meals and long conversations. Tables are laid with care, food is brought to the table, and the room fills with the sound of people enjoying themselves.

The Gardens

Manicured greenery, fresh air and room to breathe. Step outside for photographs, a quiet moment together, or a few minutes away from the music with a guest you have not seen in years.

Your Wedding Day

Intimate by design.
Generous in feeling.

Ashden Manor suits couples who want a wedding that feels personal rather than performative. The scale is deliberately human: big enough to gather everyone you love, small enough that you can actually spend time with them.

  • Exclusive use of the manor and gardens — the whole place is yours.
  • A full-day celebration, from ceremony through to the evening party.
  • A private dressing room for calm, unhurried preparations.
  • A pet-friendly venue, so four-legged family members can be part of the day.
  • Spaces designed for meaningful time with guests, not rushing between rooms.
Food & Hospitality

Gather round.
Stay a little longer.

Food is part of the experience here, not an afterthought. In-house catering means the meal is woven into the rhythm of the day — relaxed enough for conversation, thoughtful enough to feel like a celebration.

Guests sit together around shared tables. Plates are passed. Wine is poured. The room settles into the comfortable hum of people enjoying themselves, and the meal becomes one of the memories that stays with you long after the last dance.
